What the video covers

The first half is design-time work: parse the model, run response-time analysis, show the 361 µs worst-case slack, then remove priority inheritance and watch the control task miss by 538 µs. The simulation exposes the mechanism — a 900 µs critical section, a middle-priority thread, and four inversions.

The second half is the target loop: generated QNX C, the SDP build, a standalone QNX6 BIOS image, and a QEMU guest that reports platform: qnx and scheduling: enforced. The close names the gaps rather than rounding them away.

What the QNX evidence is — and is not

The x86_64 harness was compiled with QNX SDP 8.0.6 / qcc 12.2.0, packaged into its own QNX6 BIOS image, and booted in QEMU TCG on one vCPU. That is runtime evidence in an emulator, not a hardware timing measurement. AArch64 runtime, QSTI package installation, and hardware-level timing remain explicitly open. The analytical and simulated numbers in the first half are model outputs, not target traces.
